Before you order

How do I order?

Choose your quantities above and tap “Order via WhatsApp”. We'll confirm availability and arrangements by message. An enquiry isn't a confirmed order until we reply.

Do you offer collection or delivery?

Collection can be arranged in Dublin 22. Delivery may also be possible depending on location, with any fee agreed before you confirm.

Can I ask for a sample?

Absolutely! Message us to ask if a free sample is available.

What about ingredients and allergens?

Our classic salame contains biscuits, chocolate/cocoa and other ingredients. Please message us for the complete ingredient and allergen information before ordering, especially if you have an allergy.
